# Test and verify

Choose evidence that matches the actual claim: automated tests, compilation, static analysis, runtime observation, screenshots, data checks or human evaluation. Do not force one universal test plan or output format.

Trace failures to observable causes and distinguish product defects, test defects and environment failures. Do not reinterpret a failing command as success or rely on an LLM's self-review.

When the expected behavior encodes a product or risk decision that has not been made, present the conflict to the human instead of silently choosing an assertion. Report exactly what ran, what passed, what failed and what remains unverified.
