# Best-practice digest

Use the user's real work and the measurement principles in `Knowledge/最佳实践/提效度量口径.md`. A claimed improvement needs a comparable baseline, a clear calculation and inspectable evidence.

AI may collect and calculate evidence but must not invent missing baseline values or decide that a result is worth organizational adoption. Match the audience and requested medium; do not require a fixed report bundle or feedback artifact.
