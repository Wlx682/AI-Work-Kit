# Creative capture

Search fresh first-hand signals across industries before selecting one opportunity. Distinguish observed market evidence from the proposed business inference, and explain why the timing, buyer and transaction path are plausible.

The user's profession can shape execution advice but must not prematurely narrow discovery. AI proposes the opportunity; the human decides appetite, ethics, capital and real-world commitment. Produce only the depth and format requested, with direct sources for time-sensitive claims.
